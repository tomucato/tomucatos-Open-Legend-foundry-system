{{!-- Feats whose prerequisites were saved before grouping existed and now read differently. Nothing is converted automatically: the GM checks each one. --}}

Why you are seeing this. The way a feat's prerequisite rows are combined has changed, so a few feats saved earlier may no longer mean what their author intended. Nothing was changed for you: only you know what each feat was meant to require.

The rule now. Each row is added to everything above it with its own "and" / "or". It does not matter whether the row is an attribute, a feat or a custom text.

Why groups were needed. "and" / "or" alone cannot say which rows belong together. The same four rows can mean two different things:

No groups
Agility 5
andMight 5
orLogic 6
andPresence 6
Reads: ((Agility 5 and Might 5) or Logic 6) and Presence 6
Presence 6 is always required.
Two groups
Agility 5
andMight 5 group with row above
orLogic 6
andPresence 6 group with row above
Reads: (Agility 5 and Might 5) or (Logic 6 and Presence 6)
Either pair is enough.

What to do. For each feat below, compare "Read before" with "Reads now".

  • If "Reads now" is not what you meant: press Open, edit the Details tab, and tick group with row above on the rows that belong with the row above them. The "Reads:" line on the feat sheet updates as you go.
  • If "Reads now" is fine: press Keep as it reads now.
